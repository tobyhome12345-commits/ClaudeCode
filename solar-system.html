How real is it? The planets start where they actually are today. Their orbit shapes, orbit tilts, axis tilts and relative speeds are real, and the Sun lights them from the middle so each one has a day side and a night side. Three things are faked so you can see it all: distances and sizes are squished (really, Neptune is 77 times farther out than Mercury), the spinning is slowed down, and the surfaces are computer-generated patterns, not photos, so do not use this Earth as a map.
